Why UkemiXR

A place people can visit, without the trip

Photos flatten a space and video decides where to look. A walkable world lets every customer explore the real place on their own terms — and remember it.

Win the customer before the visit

Buyers, tenants and guests walk the actual space from anywhere, at any hour. The ones who book a visit arrive already convinced.

Share a place like a link

Send it in a message, put it on a listing, embed it on your site. It opens instantly on a computer — nothing to install.

Presence a video can’t give

Visitors move at their own pace, stop where they want and feel the real scale of the room. Walls and furniture are solid, just like being there.

Comfortable, even on day one of VR

Our movement system keeps the view steady, so first-time visitors stay in the experience instead of taking the headset off.

How it works

From your space to a shareable link

  1. 1We capture your space

    Our team scans it on site in a single visit — rooms, gardens, whole buildings.

  2. 2We build your world

    You get a walkable version of the real place, with floors, walls and furniture that behave like the real thing.

  3. 3You share one link

    Your audience walks it on a computer, a phone or a VR headset. Update it whenever the space changes.
