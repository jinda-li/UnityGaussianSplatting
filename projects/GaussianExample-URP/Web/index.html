Why it doesn’t make you sick

Motion sickness comes from continuous visual motion your body doesn’t feel. UkemiXR’s locomotion never glides the camera and never turns it for you — the same system we’ve tuned in Unity.

  • Standing: first personYour headset sits in the avatar’s head, room-scale 1:1.
  • Stick: third personThe avatar walks; the camera holds and cuts every 0.25 s.
  • Turning: 35° snapsWhile walking it orbits the avatar. Never a smooth spin.
  • Let go: back in your headOne short fade back to first person.

From one file to a space you can walk

All in the browser, in seconds.
